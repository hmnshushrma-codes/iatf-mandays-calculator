Notes on methodology
  • Apportionment — SA-RSL employees distributed to Manufacturing Sites by FTE ratio per IATF Rules 6th Ed., Section 5.
  • Effective FTE — core + support + (repetitive × factor) + (contract × factor). Per IAF MD 5:2023.
  • Shift adjustment — +10% per additional shift, accounting for off-hours verification needs.
  • Minimums — MS audit ≥ 1.5 days · SA-RSL audit ≥ 0.5 day each · Off-site planning = 0.5 day (mandatory).
  • Reduction cap — total reduction ≤ 30% per IATF Rules 6th Ed., Section 5.3–5.4.
  • Audit type multipliers — Surveillance = ⅓ · Recertification = ⅔ of initial audit duration.
